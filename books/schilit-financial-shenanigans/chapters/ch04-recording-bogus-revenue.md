# Chapter 4: Earnings Manipulation Shenanigan No. 2 — Recording Bogus Revenue

## Core Idea
Cash movement, invoices, or counterparties do not establish a genuine customer sale. Analyze economic substance, independence, and the origin of receipts.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Four bogus-revenue tests**: identify transactions without economic substance, non-arm's-length arrangements, receipts from non-revenue activity, and genuine transactions recorded at inflated amounts.
- **Receivable corroboration**: false sales often create false or uncollectible receivables; compare receivable growth, days sales outstanding, subsequent payment, and side agreements.

## Key Concepts
- **Economic substance**: a transaction changes the parties' actual risks and benefits.
- **Arm's-length process**: independent counterparties negotiate on commercial terms.
- **Side agreement**: an undisclosed concession that can undo the apparent sale.
- **Round-trip transaction**: reciprocal transactions that may manufacture revenue without value creation.
- **Gross versus net revenue**: whether the company acts as principal or agent changes the reported top line.

## Mental Models
- Ask **would this customer buy on the same terms without the seller's financing, investment, or reciprocal purchase?**
- Use **cash-source tracing**: distinguish customer payment from borrowing, rebates, capital receipts, and money routed through affiliates.

## Anti-patterns
- **Invoice as proof**: documentation can be contradicted by secret terms or lack of payment duty.
- **Grossing up pass-through flows**: this can exaggerate scale without changing economics.

## Worked Example
Peregrine Systems recorded software-license sales to resellers even though side agreements removed their obligation to pay. Employees called the practice “parking” transactions. The book reports a later restatement that cut previously reported revenue substantially. The analyst's test is to read reseller terms and side letters, examine subsequent cash, and ask whether demand came from end users or from quarter-end accounting needs.

## Detection Matrix
| Apparent sale | Substance question | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Sale to reseller or affiliate | Is payment independent of resale or seller support? | Side agreements, financing, collections, end-customer demand |
| Reciprocal purchase | Would either purchase exist on its own? | Linked contracts and cash trail |
| Cash receipt booked as revenue | Was the payer buying goods or returning capital/financing? | Transaction agreement and cash-flow classification |
| Large gross revenue | Was the company principal or agent? | Inventory risk, pricing discretion, fulfillment obligation |

Classify the transaction before choosing a ratio. Bogus revenue is a claim about economic substance, so corroboration matters more than an isolated receivable spike.

## Key Takeaways
1. Prove the buyer's independent economic obligation.
2. Trace cash receipts to their actual source and purpose.
3. Challenge sudden receivables growth alongside exceptional sales growth.
