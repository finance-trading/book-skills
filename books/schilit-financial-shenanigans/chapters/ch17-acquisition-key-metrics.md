# Chapter 17: Acquisition Accounting Shenanigan No. 3 — Manipulating Key Metrics

## Core Idea
Acquisitions can make reported or “organic” growth and adjusted earnings look stronger through selective definitions. Reconstruct the actual combined-company comparison.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Representative sales-growth calculation**: use pro forma prior-period revenue including the target or subtract the target's post-close contribution from current revenue; label whichever method and data gaps are used.
- **Definition audit**: compare how management defines “organic,” “core,” and “pro forma” growth each period; check overlapping products, acquired revenue streams, currency, and discontinued activities.
- **Adjusted-earnings gap test**: when GAAP profit lags management's adjusted result, quantify each adjustment and its recurrence.

## Key Concepts
- **GAAP acquisition inclusion**: target results enter consolidated sales only from the closing date.
- **Pro forma revenue**: hypothetical combined-company revenue for comparable prior periods.
- **Organic growth**: growth attributed to pre-existing activities; definitions differ.
- **Core sales**: management-selected scope that must be disclosed and reconciled.
- **Adjusted earnings**: income after management's exclusions, often deal-related.

## Mental Models
- Ask **what would growth be if both periods contained the same businesses?**
- Treat management's growth definition as **a claim to reproduce**, not a standardized metric.

## Anti-patterns
- **Comparing raw consolidated growth after a large deal** with pre-deal growth.
- **Accepting “organic” as self-explanatory** without reading its definition.

## Worked Example
The book advises using a buyer's pro forma footnote to calculate year-over-year growth on the same collection of businesses. If the company instead gives the acquired target's current revenue contribution, subtract it to estimate legacy growth. If neither disclosure exists, present growth as not directly comparable rather than inventing a precise organic rate.

## Growth Comparison Table
| Available information | Calculation | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Pro forma combined revenue for both years | Current combined ÷ prior combined − 1 | Comparable combined-company growth |
| Target current contribution, no full pro forma | (Current reported − target contribution) ÷ prior legacy − 1 | Approximate legacy growth; state assumptions |
| Neither target contribution nor pro forma sales | No reliable calculation | Organic growth unavailable |

Check acquired product overlap and changes to the company's own “organic” definition before comparing the result with management's metric.

## Key Takeaways
1. Hold business scope constant across periods.
2. Reconcile organic and adjusted metrics to reported GAAP figures.
3. Explicitly mark unavailable target contribution or pro forma data.
