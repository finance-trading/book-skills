# Glossary

**Accounting anchor** — Book value and near-term value-added earnings supported by evidence, separated from speculative growth (Ch 1, 4).

**Asset turnover (ATO)** — Sales divided by net operating assets; operating-asset efficiency in the RNOA decomposition (Ch 7).

**Book-to-price (B/P)** — Reported equity book value relative to market price, jointly affected by expected ROE and risk (Ch 2, 12).

**Clean surplus** — Reconciliation of ending equity to opening equity, earnings, and net dividends (Ch 3).

**Combined ratio** — Insurer losses plus underwriting expenses relative to premiums (Ch 11).

**Contingent liability** — Possible obligation conditional on a future event; may require a conservative investor adjustment (Ch 9).

**Default point** — Forecast moment when debt service exceeds internally available cash and feasible financing alternatives (Ch 13).

**Deferred revenue** — Cash received before the related performance obligation is fulfilled (Ch 6, 10).

**Earnings yield (E/P)** — Earnings divided by price, the inverse of P/E; not a risk-free return (Ch 2, 12).

**Fair-value hierarchy** — Level 1 market prices, Level 2 comparable prices, and Level 3 model estimates (Ch 9).

**Financing leverage (LEV)** — Net financing debt divided by common equity (Ch 5).

**Float** — Premiums received before insurance claims are paid, available for investment but owed to policyholders (Ch 11).

**Free cash flow (FCF)** — Cash from operations minus net cash investment; central to liquidity, not sufficient to measure value added (Ch 3, 13).

**Growth-return profile** — Scenario map of returns under alternative growth and profitability outcomes (Ch 8).

**Implied growth (g)** — Residual-earnings growth rate that makes an accounting valuation equal a given market price under stated assumptions (Ch 4).

**Margin of safety** — Price cushion against adverse outcomes and measurement uncertainty (Ch 8, 11).

**Market's price of growth** — Market price minus a no-growth accounting value (Ch 4).

**Net debt (ND)** — Financing debt liabilities minus debt assets (Ch 5).

**Net operating assets (NOA)** — Operating assets minus operating liabilities (Ch 5).

**No-growth value** — Accounting anchor when long-run residual earnings growth beyond the explicit horizon is zero (Ch 4).

**Operating liability leverage** — Reduction of invested NOA through operating obligations such as payables or deferred revenue (Ch 6).

**Option overhang** — Potential transfer from current shareholders when options or convertibles issue shares below fair value (Ch 9).

**Price-to-book (P/B)** — Market price divided by equity book value; affected by expected ROE and its risk (Ch 2, 12).

**Price-to-earnings (P/E)** — Market price divided by current or forecast earnings; jointly reflects growth and risk (Ch 2).

**Profit margin (PM)** — Sustainable operating income from sales divided by sales (Ch 7).

**Required return (r)** — Investor's hurdle for time and risk, used as a capital charge (Ch 2, 8).

**Residual earnings (RE)** — Earnings after a required return charge on opening equity book value (Ch 3).

**Residual operating income (ReOI)** — After-tax operating earnings after a required return charge on opening NOA (Ch 6, 7).

**Return on net operating assets (RNOA)** — After-tax operating income divided by beginning NOA (Ch 6).

**Safe balance sheet** — Conservatively assessed net assets with no material hidden obligations or fragile reported values (Ch 9).

**Sustainable operating income** — Predictive operating earnings after removing transitory and non-operating items (Ch 7, 10).

**Value trap** — Low multiple that reflects genuine deterioration or risk rather than a bargain (Ch 2, 12).
