## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 第二层思考 / Second-level thinking

Ask what the consensus already believes, what the price implies, and why your view is both different **and more accurate**. A good company is not automatically a good investment. If the “easy” favorable facts are fully priced, future disappointment can dominate the result. See [ch01](chapters/ch01-second-level-thinking.md), [ch02](chapters/ch02-efficient-market-limits.md).

### 价值与价格 / Value and price

Build a defensible estimate or range of intrinsic value before using market price as a decision signal. Buying below value is the most dependable profit route Marks identifies; raising leverage, waiting for a less rational buyer, or extrapolating growth are weaker routes. The discount is useful only if the value estimate survives adverse facts and the investor can hold long enough. See [ch03](chapters/ch03-intrinsic-value.md), [ch04](chapters/ch04-price-value.md).

### 理解、识别与控制风险 / Understand, recognize, control risk

Separate probability from outcome and permanent loss from mere price volatility. At purchase, examine downside severity, leverage, refinancing, liquidity, and the chance of forced selling. Risk often grows when investors feel safest: cheap credit, rising prices, and shrinking risk premiums can hide it. A high return in a favorable market is weak evidence of skill. See [ch05](chapters/ch05-risk.md), [ch06](chapters/ch06-confirm-risk.md), [ch07](chapters/ch07-control-risk.md).

### 周期与钟摆 / Cycles and the pendulum

Trace how strong returns attract capital, weaken lending standards, and compress risk compensation, then how losses reverse the process. Read the *current* stage from price, credit terms, leverage, and investor behavior; Marks does not claim one can reliably call the exact turning point. Increase caution when optimism is extreme and prepare to act when forced selling and pessimism dominate. See [ch08](chapters/ch08-cycles.md), [ch09](chapters/ch09-pendulum.md), [ch15](chapters/ch15-cycle-position.md).

### 反向、耐心与不知 / Contrarianism, patience, and not knowing

Contrarianism is a valuation discipline, not an instruction to oppose every crowd. Look where perception is worse than reality, verify that the risk is priced, and keep the ability to survive being early. Do not manufacture opportunity in a low-return market. Treat macro forecasts as uncertain; use alternative histories to judge whether past success required luck. See [ch11](chapters/ch11-contrarian.md), [ch12](chapters/ch12-cheap-assets.md), [ch13](chapters/ch13-patience.md), [ch14](chapters/ch14-prediction-limits.md), [ch16](chapters/ch16-luck.md).

### 防御与增值 / Defense and value added

Keep defense inside portfolio construction at all times: price discipline, position size, liquidity, and limited leverage. Judge performance across cycles after adjusting for market exposure and investing style. A manager who earns twice the benchmark in up years and loses twice as much in down years may only have high beta, not alpha. See [ch17](chapters/ch17-defensive.md), [ch18](chapters/ch18-avoid-traps.md), [ch19](chapters/ch19-add-value.md), [ch21](chapters/ch21-important-things.md).
