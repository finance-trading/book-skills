# Glossary

**Accretion/dilution** — Change in buyer earnings per share after a transaction; it does not establish whether the deal creates economic value (Ch 7).

**Cash sweep** — Financing provision directing excess cash to repay debt, subject to contractual rules (Ch 4–5).

**CIM** — Confidential information memorandum describing a company and its investment case to qualified buyers (Ch 4, 6).

**Comparable companies analysis** — Valuation benchmark using public-company share prices and operating metrics (Ch 1).

**Control premium** — Consideration above an unaffected market reference; it may reflect competition or expected benefits and is not pure synergy (Ch 2).

**Data room** — Controlled collection of diligence materials made available to authorized transaction parties (Ch 6–7).

**DCF** — Discounted cash flow valuation; present value of forecast cash flow and terminal value (Ch 3).

**Debt capacity** — Debt amount and structure a company can support given cash flows, collateral, risk, and lender terms (Ch 4–5).

**EBIAT / NOPAT** — Operating earnings after tax but before financing costs (Ch 3).

**Enterprise value (EV)** — Value of operating assets available to capital providers, adjusted for debt-like claims and cash (Ch 1–3, 5).

**FCFF** — Free cash flow to the firm, after operating taxes and reinvestment, available to debt and equity capital providers (Ch 3).

**Fully diluted shares** — Basic shares plus net dilutive shares from in-the-money options and other dilutive securities (Ch 1–2).

**Indication of interest (IOI)** — Preliminary expression of buyer interest and potential value, usually nonbinding (Ch 6).

**IRR** — Annualized return computed from the timing and amount of dated investment cash flows (Ch 5).

**LTM** — Last twelve months of historical financial results (Ch 1–3).

**MOIC** — Multiple of invested capital; total proceeds divided by invested equity, before adjusting for holding-period length (Ch 5).

**NWC** — Net working capital; increases generally use cash and must be included in operating forecasts (Ch 3, 5).

**Precedent transactions analysis** — Valuation benchmark based on acquisition prices and target financial metrics (Ch 2).

**Sources and uses** — Schedule matching transaction funding sources with the uses of that funding (Ch 4–5, 7).

**Standalone value** — Target value under its own ownership and operating plan, before buyer-specific synergies (Ch 7).

**Synergy** — Incremental benefits from combination, net of realization costs, timing, risk, and dis-synergies (Ch 2, 7).

**Terminal value** — Estimated value at the end of explicit forecast period for subsequent cash flows (Ch 3, 5).

**Treasury-stock method (TSM)** — Option dilution convention that nets in-the-money option shares against hypothetical repurchases funded by exercise proceeds (Ch 1–2).

**WACC** — Weighted average cost of capital; discount rate convention for FCFF in a DCF (Ch 3).
