# Glossary

**Abnormal return** — Return beyond a specified expected-return benchmark (Ch 6).

**Adjusted present value (APV)** — Unlevered operating value plus financing tax benefits less expected distress costs (Ch 15).

**Asset turnover** — Sales generated per unit of operating assets or invested capital (Ch 11).

**Beta** — Sensitivity of security returns to the chosen market portfolio, not total business volatility (Ch 4, 8).

**Book equity** — Accounting residual of assets less liabilities; not a direct estimate of market equity value (Ch 3, 19, 21).

**Bottom-up beta** — Business-risk beta inferred from peer assets, weighted for business mix, then relevered (Ch 8, 24).

**Capital charge** — Required return on capital committed to a business for a period (Ch 32).

**Competitive advantage period (CAP)** — Span over which a firm can maintain returns above its cost of capital (Ch 12, 31).

**Contingent claim** — Right with payoff depending on another asset and an exercise condition (Ch 2, 5).

**Cost of capital (WACC)** — Market-value-weighted required return on debt and equity used for FCFF (Ch 8, 15).

**Cost of equity (Ke)** — Required return for equity risk used to discount dividends or FCFE (Ch 4, 14).

**Country risk premium** — Incremental compensation for exposure to sovereign or country conditions, scaled to firm exposure (Ch 7, 8).

**Default spread** — Yield premium over default-free debt for credit loss risk (Ch 4, 7).

**Economic value added (EVA)** — After-tax operating income less a cost-of-capital charge on invested capital (Ch 32).

**Enterprise value** — Value of operations available to capital providers before allocation to debt and equity (Ch 2, 15–20).

**Equity risk premium (ERP)** — Required market equity return above a matching risk-free rate (Ch 7).

**Excess return** — Return on capital above the matching required return (Ch 19, 21, 31–32).

**FCFE** — Cash flow available to equity after operating needs, reinvestment, and net debt financing (Ch 10, 14).

**FCFF** — Cash flow available to debt and equity after operating taxes and reinvestment, before financing flows (Ch 10, 15).

**Firm value** — Value of operating assets before subtracting financial claims (Ch 2, 15–16).

**Implied ERP** — Equity premium solved from today's market index price and forecast aggregate cash flows (Ch 7).

**Intrinsic value** — Cash-flow-based estimate under an explicit operating and risk scenario (Ch 1–2).

**Marginal ROIC** — Expected return on newly invested capital; may differ from historical ROIC (Ch 11–12).

**Margin of safety** — Price discount to estimated value intended to absorb model and outcome error (Ch 1, 33).

**Net operating loss (NOL)** — Tax loss carried forward to offset future taxable income subject to tax rules (Ch 10).

**Noncash working capital** — Operating current assets less operating current liabilities, excluding financing cash/debt (Ch 10).

**Option to abandon** — Right to stop a project and collect salvage or avoid later losses (Ch 29).

**Option to delay** — Right to postpone an irreversible investment while retaining future upside (Ch 28).

**Option to expand** — Right to scale a project after favorable information or market development (Ch 29).

**Price-to-book (P/B)** — Equity market value divided by book equity, driven by expected ROE relative to Ke (Ch 19, 21).

**Pricing/relative valuation** — Inference from comparables' market prices after normalizing fundamentals (Ch 2, 17).

**Regulatory capital** — Required equity buffer supporting a financial firm's risk-weighted activities (Ch 21).

**Reinvestment rate** — Share of after-tax operating income invested in net capex and operating working capital (Ch 11–12).

**Return on invested capital (ROIC)** — After-tax operating income divided by operating invested capital (Ch 3, 11).

**Risk-free rate** — Default-free base rate in the cash flow's currency and matching time basis (Ch 7).

**Sales-to-capital ratio** — Incremental sales supported by one unit of new capital (Ch 11, 23).

**Status-quo value** — Value under current management and strategy before feasible control or merger changes (Ch 25, 34).

**Terminal value** — Value at the end of the explicit forecast of later cash flows (Ch 12, 14–15).
