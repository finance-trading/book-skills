# Chapter 15: 演讲

## Core Idea
Fear before a new challenge can be reduced through preparation, supportive people, and evidence of prior capability. The success journal supplies facts that counter catastrophic self-talk.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Prepare, don’t memorize rigidly**: Know the core points and practice answering likely questions so delivery remains natural.
- **Evidence review**: Before performing, review documented successes and the actions that produced them.
- **Support system**: Trusted people can reduce anxiety, but preparation remains the speaker’s responsibility.

## Key Concepts
- **Performance anxiety**: A normal response to visibility and uncertainty.
- **Self-efficacy**: Confidence grounded in prior successful effort.
- **Practice**: Rehearsal converts vague fear into a manageable sequence.

## Mental Models
- Replace “I cannot do this” with “What is the next prepared step?”
- Use prior evidence without pretending it guarantees the outcome.
- Ask for support early rather than isolating until the event.

## Anti-patterns
- **Catastrophic rehearsal**: Repeatedly imagining only failure increases fear.
- **Avoidance**: Hoping illness or cancellation removes the challenge prevents skill growth.

## Worked Example
Before speaking to parents, the protagonist reviews her journal: she found work, opened an account, handled fear, and helped initiate a financial conversation. She then uses a question-and-answer structure practiced with a teacher rather than reading a rigid script.

## Key Takeaways
1. Prepare the message and likely questions.
2. Review real evidence of capability when afraid.
3. Accept support while retaining responsibility for the task.
