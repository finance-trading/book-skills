# Chapter 11: 爸爸妈妈不明白

## Core Idea
Effort and learning can change more than income: the protagonist becomes curious, capable, and engaged. Confidence is strongest when the success journal records why an action worked, not just that it happened.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Success review**: Record the action, the reason it succeeded, and what can be repeated.
- **Preparation plus effort**: Treat apparent luck as the product of readiness, persistence, and useful relationships.

## Key Concepts
- **Reflective practice**: Reviewing experience turns events into reusable learning.
- **Intrinsic motivation**: Enjoyment and purpose sustain work that is difficult.
- **Agency**: Taking a next step can improve one’s outlook even before the situation is solved.

## Mental Models
- Ask “what did I do that made this outcome more likely?”
- Distinguish courage from absence of fear: action can continue despite fear.
- Learn subjects more deeply when they connect to a personally relevant goal.

## Anti-patterns
- **Calling preparation mere luck**: This hides the choices that can be repeated.
- **Recording only outcomes**: Without causes, a journal cannot guide future action.

## Worked Example
After a frightening adventure, the protagonist identifies how a goal led her to seek work, build relationships, and learn. In the journal she records both success and the causes behind it, including persistence despite fear.

## Key Takeaways
1. Review causes, not only results.
2. Useful effort can improve confidence and engagement.
3. Do not confuse a success story with proof that every outcome is controllable.
