# Chapter 18: 大冒险的结局

## Core Idea
Sustainable progress combines delegation, continued saving, patience through volatility, and a natural attitude toward money. Success is iterative: difficulties reveal which tasks to learn, systematize, or hand to others.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Delegate repeatable work**: Keep tasks requiring one’s distinctive skill and transfer suitable tasks to capable helpers.
- **Maintain the allocation**: Continue separating long-term capital, goal savings, and discretionary spending as income grows.
- **Hold through a tested plan**: Avoid selling merely to “take profits” when the money would return to the same suitable long-term investment.

## Key Concepts
- **Systems**: Records and simple processes make growth manageable.
- **Iteration**: Obstacles can identify the next skill to learn.
- **Normalized money**: Discuss and manage money calmly rather than treating it as taboo or magical.

## Mental Models
- Ask which work requires you and which can be taught.
- Let the original time horizon govern temporary price moves.
- Keep saving rules stable while reviewing them when life circumstances change.

## Anti-patterns
- **Doing everything personally**: Growth stalls when the founder retains every task.
- **Selling without a better use**: Realizing gains only to reinvest identically may add cost without benefit.
- **Assuming narrative outcomes are typical**: The story’s smooth business and family turnaround is illustrative, not evidence.

## Worked Example
The protagonist’s dog-care work grows beyond her time, so she delegates walks and grooming while learning recordkeeping. The club’s fund falls for months; because its purpose and horizon remain unchanged, the members do not sell. They continue regular contributions and their allocation plan.

## Key Takeaways
1. Delegate work that others can perform well.
2. Keep goal-based saving consistent as income changes.
3. Revisit investments when goals change, not merely when prices fluctuate.
