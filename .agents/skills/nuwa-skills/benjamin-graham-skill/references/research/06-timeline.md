# Benjamin Graham Research: Timeline

## Scope

This timeline covers Benjamin Graham from birth through death, then replaces any "recent 12 months" lens with "posthumous legacy and current relevance as of 2026-08-24." It is written for a generated huashu-nuwa perspective skill, so the emphasis is not only what happened, but how each episode shaped Graham's operating model: disciplined evidence, margin of safety, Mr. Market, the separation of investing from speculation, and late-life humility about professional stock selection.

Evidence labels:

- Primary: Graham's own works/interviews, official publisher/library records for a publication, or an institution/company documenting its own history.
- Secondary: Reputable biographies, university profiles, scholarly resource guides, or edited third-party accounts.
- Inference: A synthesis drawn from multiple dated facts; use as skill-builder framing, not as a standalone historical fact.

## Posthumous Influence

1. Buffett transmission: Buffett repeatedly used Graham's language and frameworks in Berkshire Hathaway letters, especially Mr. Market in 1987 and the claim in 2013 that he owed much of his investing knowledge to Graham. Classification: Primary. Sources: [S9](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1987.html), [S10](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2013ltr.pdf). As-of: 2026-08-24.

2. Zweig transmission: Jason Zweig authored Columbia's Graham profile and later became the modern commentator/editor associated with The Intelligent Investor, helping preserve Graham's original text while updating the reader's bridge to current markets. Classification: Secondary/Primary. Sources: [S1](https://c250.columbia.edu/c250_celebrates/your_columbians/benjamin_graham.html), [S12](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/the-intelligent-investor-3rd-ed-benjamin-grahamjason-zweig). As-of: 2026-08-24.

3. Institutional transmission: Columbia's Heilbrunn Center frames value investing as beginning from Graham and Dodd's 1928 reason-based teaching, while UCLA carries Graham's name through a current undergraduate value-investing program. Classification: Primary/Secondary. Sources: [S4](https://business.columbia.edu/heilbrunn/about/valueinvestinghistory), [S13](https://economics.ucla.edu/valueinvesting). As-of: 2026-08-24.

4. Publishing transmission: Security Analysis reached a seventh edition in 2023, and The Intelligent Investor, 3rd Ed. remained actively published by HarperCollins in 2024/2026 with Jason Zweig commentary. Classification: Primary. Sources: [S11](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/mhp/product/security-analysis-seventh-edition-principles-techniques.html), [S12](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/the-intelligent-investor-3rd-ed-benjamin-grahamjason-zweig). As-of: 2026-08-24.

5. Current relevance as of 2026-08-24: Graham is not only a historical "father of value investing." He is a reusable decision discipline for environments with hype, leverage, extrapolated growth, and noisy market prices. The current skill should emphasize transferable cognitive routines: demand evidence, define intrinsic value, insist on margin of safety, use market quotations as offers rather than instructions, and revise methods when competition changes. Classification: Inference. Sources: [S9](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1987.html), [S10](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2013ltr.pdf), [S11](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/mhp/product/security-analysis-seventh-edition-principles-techniques.html), [S14](https://www.grahamanddoddsville.net/wordpress/Files/Gurus/Benjamin%20Graham/A%20Conversation%20with%20Ben%20Graham%20-%20Financial%20Analysts%20Journal%20-%201976.pdf). As-of: 2026-08-24.

## Extraction Notes for Skill Builder

- Core chronology to preserve in generated perspective: 1894 birth as Benjamin Grossbaum; 1895 family migration; Columbia 1914; Wall Street start; 1926 Graham/Newman firm; 1928 Columbia teaching with Dodd; 1929 crash; 1934 Security Analysis; 1948 GEICO; 1949 The Intelligent Investor; 1950-1956 Buffett transmission; 1956 active-management retirement; UCLA/later teaching; 1976 interview and death; 1977 CFA memorialization; 1987/2013 Berkshire uses; 2023 Security Analysis 7th edition; 2024/2026 Intelligent Investor 3rd Ed.; current relevance as of 2026-08-24.

- Voice ingredients: austere, empirical, teacherly, wary of glamour, focused on error reduction. Graham should sound like someone asking, "What is the evidence? What is the asset value? What can go wrong? What price compensates me for being wrong?"

- Avoid caricature: Do not reduce Graham to "buy statistically cheap stocks." Include his later openness to simplified rules and indexing logic, his institutional teaching role, his shareholder activism, and his non-stock intellectual range.

- Buffett distinction: Graham's influence on Buffett is central, but Buffett is not a clone. Use Graham for margin of safety, market temperament, and analytical discipline; use Buffett only as proof of transmission and adaptation.

- Skill activation prompts this timeline supports: market panic, valuation discipline, avoiding speculation, evaluating uncertain assets, deciding when not to act, separating price from value, teaching a novice investor, and assessing whether an old method still works under modern competition.
