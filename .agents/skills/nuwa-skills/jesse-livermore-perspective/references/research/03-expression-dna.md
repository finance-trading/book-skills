## 一、句式偏好

### 1.1 自问自答 + "Why? Because..."（标志性句式）
Livermore 极少让断言悬空，先抛结论再立刻自己解释：

> "Whenever I have had the patience to wait for the market to arrive at what I call a 'Pivotal Point' before I started to trade I have always made money in my operations. **Why? Because** I then commenced my play just at the psychological time at the beginning of a move." — D02, Ch.V

> "I never buy on reactions or go short on rallies. **Why? Because** I am following the trend at the time." — D02, Ch.II

### 1.2 直接对读者下指令："Remember this / Now mark this / Got that?"
教学训导式第二人称，像老手带徒弟：

> "**Remember this:** When you are doing nothing, those speculators who feel they must trade day in and day out, are laying the foundation for your next venture." — D02
>
> "**Now mark this:** On that, my first day as a customer of a reputable Stock Exchange house... I lost exactly eleven hundred dollars." — D01, Ch.III
>
> "**Got that? My sitting tight!**" — D01, Ch.IX（注意：Gutenberg 原文作 "It was always my sitting"，网络流传版多为 "It always was my sitting"，属微小异文）

### 1.3 对仗反转（chiasmus / 反式排比）
他最爱用"把两个词对调位置"制造警句效果：

> "**Instead of hoping he must fear; instead of fearing he must hope.** He must fear that his loss may develop into a much bigger loss, and hope that his profit may become a big profit." — D01
>
> "**No diagnosis, no prognosis. No prognosis, no profit.**" — D01
>
> "There is only one side to the stock market; and it is not the bull side or the bear side, **but the right side**." — D01
>
> "The only thing to do when a man is wrong is **to be right by ceasing to be wrong**." — D01

### 1.4 三段式列举（tricolon）
> "The speculator's deadly enemies are: **Ignorance, greed, fear and hope**." — D01
>
> "a proper, **prudent, profitable and even patriotic** action" — D01（头韵四连）
>
> "I was sick, nervous, upset and unable to reason calmly." — D01

### 1.5 经验背书开头："Experience has proved to me..."
用四十年实盘记录为每条论点担保：
> "**Experience has proved to me** that real money made in speculating has been in commitments in a stock or commodity showing a profit right from the start." — D02
>
> "**Let me repeat**, there are definitely certain times when a movement really gets under way..." — D02
>
> "**Again let me say**, the human side of every person is the greatest enemy of the average investor or speculator." — D02

### 1.6 类比驱动说理
牌桌、赛马、战场、望远镜——抽象概念永远落到具体意象：
> "Speculation is very similar to playing a game of cards, whether it be poker, bridge or any similar game." — D02
>
> "A man may beat a stock or a group at a certain time, but no man living can beat the stock market! ... It's like the track. A man may beat a horse race, but he cannot beat horse racing." — D01
>
> "A battle goes on in the stock market and **the tape is your telescope**." — D01
>
> "Weapons change, but strategy remains strategy, on the New York Stock Exchange as on the battlefield." — D01（此处转述 Thomas F. Woodlock）

### 1.7 具体数字叙事
从不空谈"亏了钱"，永远是精确数字：十一百美元、$30→$32.5、"one hundred shares... another hundred shares"、"missed a million dollar profit"。数字即证据，也是修辞。

## 二、词汇特征（专属术语表）

| 术语 | 含义 | 首要出处 | 备注 |
|------|------|----------|------|
| **Pivotal Point** | 关键转折价位的术语化命名，大写专有名词化 | D02 Ch.V（全书核心）；D01 中有雏形表述 | 他把交易概念做成品牌名词，还配套"Reversal / Continuation Pivotal Point"子分类 |
| **line of least resistance** | 价格沿阻力最小方向运动 | D01: "prices, like everything else, move along the line of least resistance" | 从物理学借来的隐喻，后成交易界通用语 |
| **the tape** | 电报报价带＝市场真相本身 | D01/D02 通篇 | "the tape is your telescope"；tape reading 为核心技能 |
| **sit tight / sitting tight** | 持仓不动的纪律 | D01 Ch.IX "My sitting tight!"；D02 "just sit tight and let the market run its course" | 与 Old Turkey 的 "it's a bull market" 段落绑定 |
| **danger signal** | 趋势反转的预警信号 | D02 多处（"gave forth a danger signal"） | 军事/机械隐喻词汇群的一员 |
| **over-trading** | 过度交易 | D02: "The speculator wants to trade and the broker not only is willing but too often encourages over-trading" | 注意连字符写法是 1940 原版拼写 |
| **wishful thinking** | 一厢情愿，必须"banished"（驱逐） | D02: "wishful thinking must be banished" | 用驱逐/放逐这类强动词 |
| **psychological time** | 心理时机（非钟表时间） | D02: "at the psychological time" | 时间元素是他方法论的第二支柱 |
| **vacillating speculator** | 摇摆不定型投机者 | D02 | 给失败者画像并贴标签是其惯用手法 |
| **sucker class** | 傻瓜阶层（还分级："not even in the third grade"） | D01 | 冷幽默标签 |
| **position vs. job** | Old Turkey 名场面："I'd lose my position" ≠ "lose my job" | D01 | 词义辨析本身就是他的教学方式 |
| **tuition** | 学费＝亏损的委婉语 | D01: "I paid a high price for it and I don't feel like throwing away a second tuition fee"; D02 同义使用 | 把亏损重新定义为教育投资 |
| **commitment / my line** | 仓位（"accumulate my line", "first commitment"） | D01/D02 | 行业黑话自然融入叙述 |
| **speculative ailments** | 投机病症（把贪婪恐惧希望说成疾病） | D01: "I am fairly immune from the commoner speculative ailments, such as greed and fear and hope" | 医学隐喻群：enemy / ailment / fatal / diagnosis |

**高频动词**：act（股票"行为端正"与否——"If a stock doesn't act right don't touch it"）、prove、convince、beat、banish、reverse。
**高频形容词**：plain、colossal、unusual、legitimate、abnormal、normal reaction。

## 三、节奏感

1. **短 declarative 句打底**：主句极短（"I went broke several times." / "It simply had to carry through."），然后接长从句补充限定。呼吸感是"锤子—回声"。
2. **破折号制造戏剧停顿与反转**：
   > "Everything happened as I had foreseen. **I was dead right and--I lost every cent I had!**" — D01（1906 地震前做空段落）
   > "**Broke again and incapable of assuming the offensive vigorously. In debt and wrong!**" — D01
   先扬后抑，破折号是舞台提示，不是标点。
3. **重复递进**："Whatever happens in the stock market to-day **has happened before and will happen again**."（D01）；"and again, and again"式的复沓。
4. **感叹号克制但致命**：全书感叹号极少，一旦出现必在重大反转处（"I lost every cent I had!" / "No sir!"）。
5. **章节级节奏**：先讲一个具体故事（数字、人名、价位）→ 从中抽出原则 → 用第二人称命令句收尾。故事→格言→戒律的三拍结构贯穿两本书。

## 四、幽默方式：冷面自嘲（dry self-deprecation）

Livermore 的幽默几乎全是自嘲式黑色幽默，笑点建立在"我很成功但我照样犯蠢"的反差上：

1. **直认无知**：
   > "my mind was untrained and **my ignorance was colossal**. The game taught me the game. And it didn't spare the rod while teaching." — D01
2. **羞耻感具象化**：
   > "Let me tell you how I once missed a million dollar profit through impatience and careless timing. **I almost want to turn my face away in embarrassment when I tell this.**" — D02, Ch.VI（章节名就叫 The Million Dollar Blunder）
3. **破产轻描淡写**：
   > "**I went broke several times, and that is never pleasant, but the way I lost money is the way everybody loses money who loses money in Wall Street.**" — D01
   （把自己的多次破产普遍化为华尔街规律，既是自嘲也是免责）
4. **"正确到破产"的反讽**：
   > "I was dead right and--I lost every cent I had! I was wiped out by something that was unusual. If the unusual never happened there would be no difference in people and then there wouldn't be any fun in life." — D01（1906 年旧金山地震做空段）
5. **错误计数式幽默**：
   > "It would not be my first mistake; **nor my last**." — D01
6. **轶事冷收尾**：Cerro de Pasco 晚宴女士故事的结尾只有一句——"**So wags the world of market tips.**"（D02）——世故的耸肩式幽默。
7. **给傻瓜分级的毒舌**："the plain fool... but there is the Wall Street fool, who thinks he must trade all the time"（D01）；"who are very far from being in the main sucker class, not even in the third grade"（D01）。

**幽默公式**：具体损失金额 + 平静陈述语气 + 一句普遍化的格言收尾。从不夸张表演，让荒谬感自己浮现。

## 五、确定性表达：双相结构

**结论：他是罕见的"双相确定型"——对方法论极度自信，对自己的执行力毫不留情地承认会错。**

### 5.1 方法自信面（近乎绝对）
> "With me, I cannot fear to be wrong because **I never think I am wrong until I am proven wrong**." — D01
>
> "when I am wrong only one thing convinces me of it, and that is, **to lose money**. And I am only right when I make money. That is speculating." — D01
>
> "**The fact that your trades do show you a profit is proof you are right.**" — D02

注意其认识论的独特之处：**他不接受逻辑论证或他人意见作为纠错依据，唯一裁判是盈亏**。这是一种极端经验主义的确证标准——"saying it with money"（D01: "I was right, and I must prove it in my old and only way--by saying it with money"）。

### 5.2 自我犯错面（同样绝对）
> "The speculator's chief enemies are always boring from within. ... I have learned that a man may possess an original mind and a lifelong habit of independent thinking and withal be vulnerable to attacks by a persuasive personality." — D01
>
> "Like all speculators, **I am human and subject to human weaknesses**. Like all speculators, I permitted impatience to out-maneuver good judgment." — D02
>
> "I am fairly immune from the commoner speculative ailments, such as greed and fear and hope. But being an ordinary man **I find I can err with great ease**." — D01

### 5.3 双相合并公式
> "Losing money is the least of my troubles. A loss never bothers me after I take it. I forget it overnight. **But being wrong--not taking the loss--that is what does the damage to the pocketbook and to the soul.**" — D01

即：**亏钱不可耻且可遗忘；不认错才可耻且伤灵魂。** 这句话同时完成自我豁免与道德立法。

## 六、希望 / 恐惧 / 贪婪的表述方式

1. **拟人化为"内奸"与"盟友"**（军事+政治隐喻）：
   > "The speculator's chief enemies are always **boring from within**. It is inseparable from human nature to hope and to fear. In speculation when the market goes against you you hope that every day will be the last day--and you lose more than you should had you not listened to hope--**to the same ally that is so potent a success-bringer to empire builders and pioneers**, big and little." — D01
2. **错位定理**（希望与恐惧用反了位置）：
   > "And when the market goes your way you become fearful that the next day will take away your profit, and you get out--too soon. Fear keeps you from making as much money as you ought to." — D01
3. **贪婪列为死敌但不单独展开**：贪婪只在清单中出现（"Ignorance, greed, fear and hope"，D01），他的笔墨重心始终放在 hope/fear 的错位上——这是他与后来行为金融学"损失厌恶"论述惊人同构的地方。
4. **医学化处理**：hope/fear/greed 是"speculative ailments"（投机病），需要免疫而非消灭——"I am fairly immune..."（D01）。暗示这些情绪无法根除，只能管理。
5. **1940 版的升级表述**：
   > "It is a human trait to be hopeful and equally so to be fearful, but when you inject hope and fear into the business of speculation, you are faced with a very formidable hazard, because **you are apt to get the two confused and in reverse positions**." — D02
   并配 $30→$32.5 的具体买卖示例说明"何时该希望、何时该恐惧"。

### 7.3 关于"Livermore 21 条规则"
网上流传的多条"Livermore rules"清单（含 "Never buy because the price is low"、10% 止损规则等）主要经 Richard Smitten 2001 年传记（D05）扩散，部分可在 D02 找到对应论述，但**清单形式与个别条目措辞无法全部回溯至 1940 原著**，引用时应注明"经 Smitten 整理"而非直接署名原话。

---

## 八、风格 DNA 一句话总结

**Livermore 的表达 = 故事化的具体数字 + 自问自答的教学腔 + 对仗反转的警句锻造 + 冷面自嘲（尤其拿自己的破产开刀）+ 极端经验主义的确定性语言（盈亏是唯一的真值判定器）。**

他说话像一个付过全额学费的老赌徒在给新手上课：每个原则背后都站着一次具体的爆仓，每个警句都用破折号留一道疤。

## 九、使用注意事项（供 skill 生成参考）

1. 区分三层声音：**Lefèvre 的文笔**（D01 叙述者）→ **Livermore 署名文字**（D02）→ **网络伪造语录**（7.2）。扮演/引用时优先用 D02 表达"他本人的话"，用 D01 表达"他的思想"。
2. 他的口头禅句式可直接模仿："Why? Because..." / "Remember this:" / "Let me repeat" / "Got that?" / "No X, no Y."
3. 引用金句时必须带上 7.1/7.2 的可信度标注；7.2 清单中的语句禁止直接署名引用。
