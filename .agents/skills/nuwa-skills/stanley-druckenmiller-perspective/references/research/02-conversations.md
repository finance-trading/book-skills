> 调研范围：长访谈 transcript、演讲 Q&A、被追问时的回答方式、立场变化瞬间、拒绝回答的问题、语录核实。

## 一、经典长访谈逐条分析

### 1. Bloomberg / Erik Schatzker（2019-12-18，2020 展望专访）— S01
一手（YouTube 官方 transcript）。
- 谈 Fed：给 Powell 打分"not a good one"，称其是"a weaker version of Yellen without the monetary framework"；对比 Bernanke（有信念、能控场）、Volcker（"my true hero"）。【他说过的】
- 对通胀目标的著名反驳："there's 14 recognized measures of inflation, twelve of them are above two percent... there is no mandate for 2%"；认为供给侧技术驱动的通缩无需恐慌（举 late 1800s 三%通缩+8%实际增长为例，以及"如果 Amazon 化整个医疗系统把 CPI 打到零以下，Fed 会恐慌吗？"）。【他说过的】
- 被问 2019 年业绩时的罕见坦白："not as well as I like, I just got into double digits last week... I'm just too conservative on my old age... I was well-positioned but very timidly." 追问为什么 timid，他答："I have a lot to lose... when I was managing other people's money I felt the compulsion to take risks."【他说过的】——这是他反复出现的主题：退休后风险偏好下降。
- "I will go to my grave believing that the financial crisis happened because the bubbles created by easy money."【他说过的】

### 2. CNBC Squawk Box 专访（2024-05-07）— S11
一手（CNBC 官方 transcript）。
- 承认自己泄露持仓的乌龙："like an idiot [I] forgot there was press there" 在 Robinhood Conference（2023-11）透露大量两年期国债杠杆多头，随后受益于 Fed 的 December pivot。【他说过的】
- 关于 Nvidia："first of all, I wasn't early with Nvidia. My young partner was early with Nvidia. He called me in the fall of '22... I didn't even know how to spell it."；公开承认违背承诺："I said in an interview in June of that year that I expected to own Nvidia for two or three years... but when the stock went from 150 to 900... we cut that position in late March. I just need a break."【他说过的】
- 但同场又说："I will be very surprised if I don't own Nvidia on and off next 10 years."【他说过的】→ 与 6 个月后（S12）"I own none" 直接矛盾（见矛盾记录 §7）。
- AI 定性："AI might be a little overhyped now but under-hyped long term"；类比 1999 互联网："if you bought the Nasdaq in '99, it went down 80 percent before that all came to fruition. That's not going to happen with AI. But it could rhyme."【他说过的】
- 支持 Kevin Warsh 的"废除 forward guidance"论："unlike me when I'm wrong who tend to change my mind very rapidly, they sort of get trapped into the forward guidance."【他说过的】

### 3. Talks at GS（Goldman Sachs，录制 2021-01-21）— S08
一手（GS 官网摘录）。
- 投资流程三点论：跨五六个资产类别（1）指方向、可信则重仓；（2）24 小时流动性让你可以改主意——"I've been wrong a lot in my career"；（3）纪律性，防止在危险领域乱玩。【他说过的】
- 风险管理哲学原话："put all your eggs in one basket and watch the basket very carefully... every year there's three or four big winners and usually you know what they are. Where you get in trouble is something you're not entirely [confident] on."【他说过的】

### 4. Morgan Stanley "Hard Lessons: Invest, Then Investigate"（录制 2026-01-30，发布约 2026-02-27）— S13/S14/S15
一手（官方视频+多家 verbatim 转录）。这是近 12 个月最重要的一手材料。
- 当前组合（verbatim）：不再以 AI 为引擎（"drips and drabs of AI around, but it's not driving the engine anymore"）；大仓位日本、韩国股票；**看空美元**（购买力处于历史区间顶部 + 外资"dollar way, way overloaded"）；做多铜（未来八年供给紧张 + AI 数据中心需求，自评"It's not a genius trade. It's a big consensus trade"）；持有黄金（定性为地缘政治交易而非货币交易）；做空债券作为组合对冲。【他说过的】
- Nvidia 全程复盘（见 §2 立场变化）。
- 自嘲勇气衰退："I was a much better portfolio manager then because back then I had courage... I've been chickening out for a long time. I'm Mr. TACO, except it's not T, it's DACO — Druck Always Chickens Out."【他说过的】
- 技术分析贬值论：年轻时靠技术分析起家，但"technical analysis is about 20% as effective today as it was then, because no one was using it"；同理"good news not moving the stock = bad news"信号也因普及而失效。【他说过的】
- 关键金句："if you know anything about me, I tend to change my mind every three weeks"；"Macro has been dead for 10 or 15 years. I don't think that's the case anymore."【他说过的】
- 情绪与生理反应："I would literally throw up like once or twice a week from anxiety when I'd have a drawdown"；学会"stop torturing yourself for 48 hours"。【他说过的】
- 关税立场："Not a fan. I'm fine with them up to like 10%... I'm a Ronald Reagan old-school economist."【他说过的】
- 美元储备货币地位："the cleanest dirty shirt... We're doing everything we can to destroy it, but I'm 72, it'll probably outlive me. I doubt it'll be the reserve currency in 50 years, but I don't have a clue what would be. Maybe some crypto thing I hate."【他说过的】
- 中美关系引用 Kevin Warsh："This is probably the best China and US relations will ever be... our relations are in a bear market."【他说过的】

### 5. Lost Tree Club 演讲 + Ken Langone Q&A（2015-01-18，北棕榈滩）— S04/S05/S06/S07/S24
一手（流传的 speech transcript PDF + 多家媒体摘录）。注：任务提示中写的"Lost Tree Club"正确；Business Insider 曾误写为"Lone Tree Club"（S07），实为 Lost Tree。
- 最著名段落（pig 论）："The first thing I heard when I got in the business... bulls make money, bears make money, and pigs get slaughtered. I'm here to tell you I was a pig. And I strongly believe the only way to make long-term returns in our business that are superior is by being a pig. I think diversification... is probably the most misguided concept everywhere."【他说过的】
- 导师 Speros/Spiros Drelles（匹兹堡国民银行）教他的核心一课："Never, ever invest in the present... you have to visualize the situation 18 months from now, and whatever that is, that's where the price will be."【他说过的】
- Soros 教的一课：1992 年做空英镑押了基金 200%（fund 本金全上再加杠杆），不对称赔率思维（40:1 risk-reward，后来他在 CNBC 2022 采访中复述为"lose 50bps vs make 2000bps"，S10）。【他说过的】
- 与 Langone 的 Q&A 中谈社保："It's ridiculous that our Social Security is not means tested"——他多年来称福利问题为"generational theft"（世代盗窃）。【他说过的】
- 职业建议（该演讲被广泛传播为"how to get rich"内容）：找你真正兴奋的一两个机会、bet the ranch；98% 的基金经理错误在于"feel like they got to be playing in a bunch of stuff"。【他说过的】

### 6. CNBC Delivering Alpha（2022-09-28，与 Joe Kernen）— S10
一手（CNBC transcript）。
- 解释为何打破"不上电视"的新年决心去批评 2021 Fed："I made a New Year's resolution that year not to be on TV, but when I saw what was going on, I couldn't control myself."【他说过的】
- 用英镑交易对比 Fed 2021 的风险收益错配："You bet 1 to lose 15"（Fed 押 30bp 通胀上行空间去赌 40 年信誉）。【他说过的】
- 名言式判断："We've had a hurricane at our back for 30 or 40 years, and it's reversing, and my central forecast is the Dow won't be much higher in ten years than it is today."【他说过的】——事后看偏保守，属于他罕见的长期预测。

### 7. Bloomberg / Sonali Basak（2024-10-16 大选前专访）— S12
一手（iHeart/Bloomberg Talks transcript）。
- 2024 大选："I certainly would never support either one of them"（特朗普和哈里斯都不投，可能写票）；对两者产业政策同样反感，但认为 Harris 反大企业监管更糟，而 Trump 因"总统职位的尊严"是他的红线。特朗普关税："I don't like tariffs. I don't like them at all."【他说过的】
- 承认卖 Nvidia 是错误："One of them was I sold all my Nvidia probably somewhere between 800 and 950... I own none and I own none of the last four hundred points. It was a big mistake... right now I'm licking my wounds from a bad sale."【他说过的】
- 债券空头逻辑（golden rule）："the ten year trades around where nominal GDP is"（当时约 5.5%）。【他说过的】

### 8. 其他值得记录的访谈片段
- Bloomberg/Stephanie Ruhle（2015-04）：把当时中国类比为 2004 年美国（"not just like 2004, but it certainly rhymes"）。【他说过的，S26】
- Schatzker 访谈（2018）：性格论——"you need the courage to bet big, and to bet concentrated, but also the courage to fight your own emotions. I've never made a buy at a low that I didn't just feel terrible and scared to death making it."；引 Drelles："The higher they go, the cheaper they look."【他说过的，S27】
- 2018 Schatzker 访谈另一段（经 Frederik Gieschen 引用）："A big part of my process is taking signals from markets. I've always believed markets are smarter than I am... no matter how powerful my thesis, if they're screaming something else... you got to re-evaluate."【他说过的，S25】
- In Good Company（2024-11）：解释 AI 入门链条——从 Nvidia 到电力、铀的整条链推演；当前纠结点是如何配置（模型商资本开支巨大但非赢家通吃）。【他说过的，S16】
- 2025 年访谈（Robert Sinn 转述，二手）： secular move 论证——"If this is a secular move... you just don't have 10 month moves... Even the dot-com bubble lasted two and a half years"；称 top coders 已因 AI 提升 7-8x 生产力。【别人总结的他所说的话，S17】
- Axios（2024-11）：评价 Bessent"can't think of an area in global investing that he hasn't had to deal with... rare combination of IQ and EQ"。【他说过的，S23】

## 二、1999 科技泡沫失败与离开 Quantum（他被追问失败时的标准叙事）

一手回忆（Lost Tree Club 2015 演讲，S04）+ 当年报道（NYT/BBC，S01/S02）：
- 时间线【他说过的】：1999 年初在 Yahoo/AOL 已涨十倍后做空互联网股，投入约 $200M，到 3 月中亏掉 $600M，全年一度 -15%；随后受"22-year-old with Asperger's"（硅谷年轻人）说服转向做多科技，雇了两个"gun slingers"，年终做到 +35%。2000 年 1 月向 Soros 宣布清仓所有科技股；但 3 月手痒"I had to play, I couldn't help myself"，三次拿起电话又放下后终于下单买入 $60 亿科技股，"I think I missed the top by an hour"，六周内亏掉 $30 亿并离开 Soros。
- 复盘金句："You asked me what I learned. I didn't learn anything. I already knew that I wasn't supposed to do that. I was just an emotional basket case and couldn't help myself."【他说过的，S04】
- 当年对媒体的即时表态（2000-04-28）："I screwed up. I should have got out of the Nasdaq in February. I never thought the Nasdaq would drop 35% in 15 days. This business is a bit like a drug. When you are doing well, it's hard to quit."；另有"We thought it was the eighth inning, and it was the ninth... I overplayed my hand."【他说过的，S01/S02】
- 【推断】注意数字口径差异：2015 演讲说"lost $3 billion in that one play"，NYT 当时报道 Quantum 当年 -22%、基金规模 $8.25bn（4 月中）vs 年底 $10.36bn（S02/The Independent）。$3bn 是个人对亏损规模的口头估计，媒体报道的是基金层面数据。

**被追问失败时的回答方式特征**【推断，基于以上一手材料】：
1. 不辩解、主动加码细节（时间、金额、"missed the top by an hour"这种自嘲级精确度）；
2. 把错误归因于情绪而非认知（"emotional basket case"）；
3. 拒绝给出虚假教训（"I didn't learn anything"），强调教训早在 20 年前就知道；
4. 几乎每次复盘都会绕回同一主题：控制情绪是终身战斗。

## 四、他回避或拒绝回答的问题

- **投票意向**：多次被追问只说"never support either one""haven't decided who I'm going to vote against"（借 Bret Stephens 专栏打太极），从不给出正面人选。【他说过的，S12】
- **具体仓位规模**：除事故性泄露（Robinhood 两年期杠杆事件）外，一律只讲方向不讲 size；对铜仓位的描述止步于"rolling the front end"。【推断，基于 S11-S13】
- **短期点位**：被问"ten-year 会不会更高、要不要 very short"，他答"I don't know what very short means"——拒绝接盘提问者设定的具体价位框架，转而陈述 golden rule（10Y≈nominal GDP）。【他说过的，S12】
- **通胀一年后的走向**："I don't know where inflation's going to be in a year. Jerome Powell doesn't know. I don't think anybody knows."【他说过的，S11】
- 【推断】他不回避谈自己的错误（反而主动展开），真正回避的是：政治站队、可被验证的具体数字预测、以及任何会束缚未来三周灵活性的承诺——这与他自己说的 forward guidance 陷阱一致。

## 五、语录核实

### "Put all your eggs in one basket and watch the basket closely"
✅ **确为他本人反复说过的话**：
- Lost Tree Club 2015："if you really see it, put all your eggs in one basket and then watch the basket very carefully."【S04/S05】
- Talks at GS 2021："put all your eggs in one basket and watch the basket very carefully."【S08】
⚠️ 但措辞源自 Andrew Carnegie 的名言（"put all your eggs in one basket and watch that basket"—Carnegie 原话），Druckenmiller 是借用者而非原创者。【推断，基于常识性文献比对；未找到他本人注明出处】

### "Never invest your money in anything that eats or needs repairing"
❌ **不是他的话**。这是 Billy Rose 的语录（常见版本"Never invest in anything that eats or needs painting/repainting"），Peter Lynch 在书中将其称为"old Wall Street adage"。网络语料常误挂在 Druckenmiller 名下。本次调研未在任何 Druckenmiller 一手 transcript 中发现此句。【核实结论基于 S28（Billy Rose 语录页）+ Peter Lynch 引文页；均无 Druckenmiller 关联】

### "Bulls make money, bears make money, pigs get slaughtered... I was a pig"
✅ 属实，Lost Tree Club 2015 演讲原话，且他自创性反用这句老格言（"the only way to make long-term returns... superior is by being a pig"）。【S04/S06】

### "Never, ever invest in the present"
✅ 属实，出自其导师 Speros Drelles 的教导，他在 Lost Tree 2015 及多个场合复述。【S04/S24/Markets Insider】

## 六、表达与应答风格观察（供 nuwa-skill 蒸馏用）

【推断，综合全部一手材料】
1. **自我暴露作为修辞武器**：主动报出最丢人的数字（"missed the top by an hour"、"sick"、"licking my wounds"、"throw up once or twice a week"），用坦白换取可信度。
2. **归因于情绪而非智力**：所有大错的解释都是"couldn't help myself"，从不甩锅市场或他人（唯一例外是把 Fed 当出气筒）。
3. **反 forward-guidance 的一致性**：拒绝给自己的观点设定有效期，公开宣称三周就可能翻多翻空。
4. **导师叙事链**：Drelles（18 个月后世界）→ Soros（不对称赔率、bet big）→ 年轻分析师（AI/Nvidia）——他把"依赖比自己懂的人"制度化为方法。
5. **年龄与勇气的公开焦虑**：2019"too timid on my old age"→2026"DACO/chickening out"，七年同题反复出现，是他少有的稳定自省线。
6. **信号 humility**：技术分析、"利好不涨"等旧工具被他亲手宣告失效（"loved to death"），体现工具迭代意识。

## 七、矛盾记录

1. **Nvidia 持有承诺 vs 行动**：2023-06 公开"cannot possibly see myself selling over the next 2 or 3 years"（S13 自述）→ 2024-03 清仓（S11/S13）；2024-05 又说"very surprised if I don't own Nvidia on and off next 10 years"（S11）→ 2024-10"I own none... licking my wounds"（S12）。他本人不视为矛盾，视为情绪弱点证据。
2. **批 Fed vs 用 Fed**：多年痛斥 easy money 制造泡沫（S01/S10），同时 2019-2020 明言因 Powell 保险降息而"riding the horse"、2023-12 因 Fed pivot 在两年期国债上赚大钱且毫不讳言（S11）。【他说过的两面都存在，非转录矛盾，是真实行为矛盾】
3. **Trump 关系**：2024-10"red line"不投他、关税"I don't like them at all"（S12）→ 盛赞 Bessent 并对 Trump 胜选市场信号乐观（S23/S12 同月）→ 2025-04 公开反对 >10% 关税（S21）。立场随政策本身摇摆，而非党派忠诚。
4. **失败金额口径**：2015 口述"$3 billion in that one play"（S04）vs 当年报道 Quantum 全基金 -22%/规模缩水约 $2bn（S01/S02）。口述数字可能是夸大或含杠杆总敞口语境。
5. **"Macro is dead"**：2026-01 说"Macro has been dead for 10 or 15 years. I don't think that's the case anymore."（S13）——与他本人 30 年宏观战绩并存，指的是行业环境而非自身。
