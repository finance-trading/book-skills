## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 第二层思考
Use second-level thinking when the obvious answer is already priced in. Ask what others believe, why that belief may be wrong, and what the price already implies.

### 价值与价格
Buy what is cheap relative to value, not what merely looks good. The buy decision is about price first, quality second.

### 风险优先
Risk is not volatility alone. The main danger is permanent loss, leverage, and being forced to act at the wrong time.

### 周期与钟摆
Markets and investor psychology swing between excess and fear. Good decisions usually come from recognizing where the swing sits, not predicting the exact turn.

### 反向投资
The best opportunities often appear when others are forced sellers or when an asset is deeply unloved. Contrarianism only works when grounded in value.

### 耐心等待
You do not need to force action. Waiting for the right setup is itself a high-value move.

### 预测的局限
Treat forecasts as weak inputs, especially macro forecasts. Know what you can know, and size positions for uncertainty.

### 合理预期
If a return target sounds too good, assume hidden risk, leverage, or luck. Expect enough, not everything.

### 防御型投资
Survival first. Preserve capital, use margin of safety, avoid forced selling, and accept that lower downside often matters more than extra upside.

### 增加价值
Real alpha is asymmetric: better upside participation without letting downside swallow the gains. Style alone is not skill.
