# Glossary

**15-point test** — Fisher’s qualitative screen for durable growth, management, finance, and integrity (Ch 2).
**Capital dilution** — issuing shares to fund growth in a way that reduces existing holders’ claim (Ch 2).
**Competitive advantage** — a company-specific capability that supports better growth or margins (Ch 4).
**Earnings curve** — the path of future earnings, not merely historical EPS (Ch 1, 4).
**Growth stock** — a company with unusually strong prospects for multi-year sales and earnings growth (Ch 1).
**Management depth** — enough capable leaders below the top executive to scale and survive succession (Ch 2).
**Margin** — operating profit as a share of sales; study its level and trend (Ch 2, 4).
**Scuttlebutt** — disciplined external investigation through customers, competitors, suppliers, and experts (Ch 3).
**Sell thesis** — the factual reason the original investment case no longer holds (Ch 6).
**Temporary problem** — an operating setback that obscures, but does not destroy, long-term earning power (Ch 5).
**Value of retained earnings** — the return management can generate by reinvesting cash instead of paying dividends (Ch 7).
